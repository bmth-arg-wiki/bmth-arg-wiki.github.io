# Unbeleevable by Dr. Lee MD

Unbeleevable is a series by [Dr. Lee MD](../characters/lee-md), broadcast through the fictional platform 
Community Resource Analog Programming (CRAP). Lee set out to expose the [Cult of Genxsis](church), and to 
understand how [Nex Gen](nex-gen-corporation) came to be, he winds the story right back to the [Cult of Mantra](mantra) 
and its leader [Skys](../characters/skys).

In-universe, the series walks through the history of the cult: [Project Kingslayer](incident-kingslayer), 
the scientists of the trinity, [Medea](../characters/medea)'s infiltration for [ARC/HIVE](archive) and the 
[October Massacre](october-massacre) that ended it all.

***

## Where to find the episodes

The episodes were released one at a time over different versions of the website:

- [Episode 1](../for-sof/unbeleevable) first surfaced as a video attached to a leak from Nex Gen, filed in FOR SOF.
- [Episode 2](../for-sof/unbeleevable2) was hidden behind a puzzle on the [website](../website/website).
- [Episode 3](../for-sof/unbeleevable3) premiered on YouTube, as the puzzle for it was not unlocked before the premier
- [Episode 4](../for-sof/unbeleevable4) was released with the 22 October 2026 update to the website.

On the 22nd of October 2026, the dashboard of the [Multidimensional Navigator 8](../website/website) was 
rebuilt. After this update, all the episodes are collected as videos you can watch in the media player 
on the website.
