# Radiogenic Leukotrichia

> "By now, everyone knows someone who has Radiogenic Leukotrichia or has a story of someone who does."
>
> - [The Science Study](../for-sof/thesciencestudy)

Radiogenic Leukotrichia is a condition caused by the nuclear fallout of World War 3. The radiation 
led to genetic mutations that disrupt melanin production, resulting in hair that grows without pigment 
and appears white. In extremely rare cases (approximately 1 in 5 million), a specific mutation causes 
pink hair instead.

We first learn about the condition in detail through [The Science Study](../for-sof/thesciencestudy), 
a newspaper published in [New Elysium](new-elysium) in 2037.

***

## Background

The condition results from high levels of radiation exposure. Most of the population was affected by the nuclear 
fallout of World War 3. The radiation caused genetic mutations that disrupt the production of melanin, 
the pigment that gives hair its colour. In the white-haired variant, the body reduces eumelanin (for dark hair colours) 
and pheomelanin (which produces red and yellow hues in the hair). The condition can be passed down 
from parent to child. 

In the rare pink variant, a mutation reduces the eumelanin and increases the production of 
pheomelanin. In some spiritual communities (like in India and Tibet) the pink-haired children were considered 
blessed. In [Skys](../characters/skys)' home country [Novosovia](../lore/novosovia) the condition was 
feared and individuals with the condition were treated very poorly.

***

## Notable sufferers

### Skys

[Skys](../characters/skys) was born with the pink hair variant of Radiogenic Leukotrichia in [Novosovia](novosovia). 
In the Novosovian culture at the time of Skys' youth (approximately the 90's), his condition is greatly feared. 
Skys' parents mistreat him and kick him out of the house at only 8 years old. After this, he flees the country and 
travels the world, meeting cultures that viewed the condition with a very different perspective. 

He eventually dyes his hair black with leech blood to hide the condition. We learn about Skys' early life in 
[Unbeleevable](../for-sof/unbeleevable) and [Skys' scrapbook](../website/skys-diary).

### Selene

[Selene](../characters/selene) also has the pink hair variant, as confirmed by [Sof's journal](../website/journal). 
She is referred to as NYX-01 by Nex Gen's FANTOM team. Her unique biology, including her exceptional 
pineal gland, is of great interest to [Nex Gen](nex-gen-corporation).

### Sof

[Sof](../characters/sof) suffers from the same rare pink mutation as their mother Selene and cult leader Skys. 
Sof wonders about their connection to Skys in their journal, noting that the condition appears to be 
the result of both environmental and genetic factors.
