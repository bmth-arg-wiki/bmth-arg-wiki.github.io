# Unbeleevable Episode 4

> Amid the carnage, Angel, Strassman's daughter, and the other gifted kids are taken.

Unbeleevable 4 is a new episode from [Dr. Lee MD](../characters/lee-md), continuing the story of the 
[Cult of Mantra](../lore/mantra) and the events leading up to its fall.

***

## About

This episode follows the cult in 2015: the escape of [Karl Strassman](../characters/strassman) and a heavily 
pregnant [Ava](../characters/ava) from October Palace, the birth of [Angel](../characters/angel), 
[Skys](../characters/skys)' confrontation with Strassman, [Medea](../characters/medea)'s renewed contact 
with [ARC/HIVE](../lore/archive), the founding of [Ecliptism](../lore/ecliptism) by [Alfgram](../characters/alfgram), 
and the [October Massacre](../lore/october-massacre) that brings the cult down.

The video was uploaded unlisted to [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BwtoCAwGow) on the 22nd of 
October 2026 and officially released there the following day, under the title “The Egg Doth Cometh”.
