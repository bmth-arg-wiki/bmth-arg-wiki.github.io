# Multidimensional Navigator 8 Webpage

Most of the ARG takes place in the Multidimensional Navigator 8, a fictional piece of technology that 
is also our beloved character [M8](../m8). You can access it with the following link: 

[https://www.multidimensionalnavigator8.help/index-desktop.html](https://www.multidimensionalnavigator8.help/index-desktop.html)

This is the page for the current version of the website, released on October 22nd 2026.
If you want to take a look at Version 1.0 and 2.0, from May 24th 2024 and December 4th 2024 respectively,
[click here](website-v1.md).

***

## Overview

![Version 3.0 dashboard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bmth-arg-wiki/wiki-assets/main/webpage/website-3-0.png)

Note: This newest version does not require an entry password and is accessible on mobile!
Moreover, almost all the other passwords/puzzles have been removed as well. 
[Skys' Scrapbook](skys-diary.md) and the [Transmission](../for-sof/carmine_interview.md) 
are the only things that still remain password protected.

### Spyder Web Browser - The Daily Whisper

On Sunday 15th December 2024, the access codes for the [web browser](../website/webbrowser) were found, 
prompting the discovery of this newspaper; its name being a play on the real life publication 'The Daily Mail'.
As of this date, there are 12 readable articles and 1 inaccessible article as it is overrun by pop-up ads.

There are two credited writers: [Lee M.D.](../characters/lee-md), and Gideon Mourningstone.
The columns talk about serious topics in the classic gossip way. 

A common typo is the omission of the letter combinations 'ff', 'fl', 'ffi', 'ffl' or 'fi' in certain words.
Both articles written by Lee and Gideon have this quirk, 
which leads us to believe 'Gideon Mourningstone' might be a pseudonym for Lee M.D.

You can check out all the articles by clicking their cards below.
