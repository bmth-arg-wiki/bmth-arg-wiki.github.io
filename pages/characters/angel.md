# Angel

Angel is the daughter of [Dr. Karl Strassman](strassman) and [Ava](ava), born in 2015 while the two were 
on the run from [Skys](skys) and the [Cult of Mantra](../lore/mantra). She was taken by 
[Arc/Hive](../lore/archive) as a child.

## Background

Strassman and Ava fled October Palace in 2015, with Ava heavily pregnant. They did not get far before 
labour started, and Strassman, a scientist rather than a midwife, delivered the baby himself with the help 
of [Dallus Lauren](dallus-lauren). The birth killed Ava. With her dying breath, she made Strassman promise 
to name their daughter Angel.

According to Strassman's journals (as told by [Dr. Lee MD](lee-md), these do not exist as files currently), 
Ava had sworn that an angel visited her during the pregnancy. She was absolutely convinced the baby was destined to save the world.

When [Medea](medea) re-established contact with [Arc/Hive](../lore/archive), she told them Angel had powers conventional science 
could not explain. This information, uncovered by [Feya](feya) and reported to Skys, further pushed the cult towards its collapse.

During the [October Massacre](../lore/october-massacre), [Operation Trinity](../lore/operation-trinity) extracted the three men behind Mantra for Arc/Hive. 
[Victor Carmine](../characters/victor-carmine), who had been after the gifted kids research, also had Angel and several 
other gifted children taken. Strassman was never told that Arc/Hive had taken her, and believes 
she went missing according to [Dallus' Jan 2021 diary](../for-sof/dallus-diary).
