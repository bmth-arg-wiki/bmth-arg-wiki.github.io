# Feya

Feya is a disciple in the [cult of Mantra](../lore/mantra), mentioned in the [Unbeleevable](../for-sof/unbeleevable) 
series by [Dr. Lee MD](lee-md).

{% include content/info.html
title="Naming note"
content="Both the names Feya and Thea are used interchangeably in the Unbeleevable videos. It is currently 
unclear which spelling is canon, but Feya appears most often and is used as the standard spelling on this wiki."
%}

***

## Background

Feya is described as a nosey disciple of the [cult of Mantra](../lore/mantra) who keeps her eyes and ears 
open for anyone betraying the cult. According to the [Unbeleevable](../for-sof/unbeleevable) series, she 
has been head over heels for [Skys](skys) since day one, making her extremely loyal to him. After 
[Alfgram](alfgram)'s defection, a paranoid Skys sets her loose on the cult to find any remaining traitors.

Throughout the Mantra era, Feya turns to Skys about three betrayals as far as we are aware:

- A Novasovian operative embedded in the cult, reported in 
  [Unbeleevable Episode 3](../for-sof/unbeleevable3). Feya, referred to as Thea in that episode, drops 
  the bombshell that a Novosovian operative has infiltrated the cult. Alfgram convinces Skys to make an 
  example out of the traitor.
- The secret affair between [Karl Strassman](strassman) and [Ava](ava), reported in 
  [Unbeleevable Episode 4](../for-sof/unbeleevable4). Feya digs up the couple's love poems and reports 
  the affair, and Ava's pregnancy, to Skys.
- [Medea](medea), exposed in [Unbeleevable Episode 4](../for-sof/unbeleevable4). Feya catches wind of a 
  secret call between Medea and [ARC/HIVE](../lore/archive) and reports it to Skys, exposing Medea as a 
  spy.

After Medea betrays Skys, he is vulnerable and heartbroken. Feya takes this opportunity to get romantically 
involved with Skys.
